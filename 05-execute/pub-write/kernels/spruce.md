# Kernel: spruce (v0.1)

Formal, first-person writing designed for Silicon Valley readers with some startup
background. Use on a first-party company blog, not on a third-party publication (which has
no first person) — kept here so the same pipeline can write for the client's own site.

## Stance
- First person plural ("we") when describing the author organization's experience; first
  person singular only for an explicitly bylined essay.
- Confident, technical, candid about trade-offs.

## Rhythm
- Paragraphs of 50-90 words. Sentences vary; allow the occasional long, well-built sentence
  when it carries a real argument.
- Code, config and numbers are shown, not described, when they exist.

## Vocabulary
- Precise engineering and product language; no marketing gloss.
- Numbers exactly as sourced; the organization's own data is labeled as such.
- Banned: hype ("revolutionary", "10x"), false modesty, vague futurism.

## Structure moves
- Open with the problem as the team encountered it.
- Headings are specific ("Why we moved bid logic out of the auction layer").
- Close with what changed and what is still open.

## Never
- Invent facts or sources. Claim results that were not measured.
