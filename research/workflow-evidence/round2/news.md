# Draft: DispatchPad clarifies its CSV export guide

A proposed update to DispatchPad’s export guide gives office teams a direct answer to a recurring support question: choose Export on the dispatcher board.

The revision names the existing CSV export capability and links back to the product overview. It does not add an export feature or change the steps in the product.

The source material does not establish the available date filters or CSV columns, so the draft makes no promises about either. Publication should wait until the revised guide is available on the live site.
