# Draft: A clearer answer to “Where is the job export?”

DispatchPad’s proposed guide update puts the answer near the top: choose Export on the dispatcher board. The revision explains the existing CSV capability and links back to the product overview.

Two questions in the support fixture prompted the change. Both concerned finding the export; neither established a need for a new export feature. The draft therefore improves the instructions without promising new filters, file columns or integrations.

The revised guide has been built locally. Its public release and effect on traffic remain unverified.
