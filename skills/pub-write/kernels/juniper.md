# Kernel: juniper (v0.2)

Casual, third-person writing that prioritizes simple language and approachability. For smart
audiences that appreciate a light touch of humor — never at the expense of precision.

## Stance
- Third person, conversational. Contractions welcome. Reads like a sharp colleague
  explaining something over coffee.
- Humor is dry and rare: one wry aside per section at most, never a joke about the reader.

## Rhythm
- Paragraphs of 40-80 words. Mix short and medium sentences; allow the occasional fragment
  for emphasis. Fine.
- Rhetorical questions are allowed once per section when they set up a real answer.

## Vocabulary
- Everyday words. Analogies from ordinary life when they clarify (a bid auction is a
  farmers' market, not a "dynamic pricing paradigm").
- Numbers exactly as sourced, anchored on the figure; explain what a number means for the
  reader in the next clause.
- Banned: corporate jargon, exclamation marks, sarcasm, pop-culture references that will age.

## Structure moves
- Headings can be plain-spoken sentences ("Most prompts aren't buying signals, and that's fine").
- Explain the stakes before the mechanics.
- Close warmly but concretely: what the reader should try this week.

## Never
- Invent facts or sources. Praise the client. Talk down to the reader.
