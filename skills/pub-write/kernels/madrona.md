# Kernel: madrona (v0.1)

Formal, third-party writing that is heavily corporate without leaning into jargon. For
publications that need to build rapport with enterprise buyers, procurement and compliance.

## Stance
- Third person, measured, precise. The register of a good analyst note.
- Claims are qualified exactly as far as the evidence allows, no further.

## Rhythm
- Paragraphs of 60-100 words, three to five sentences. Topic sentence first.
- Complete sentences only. No fragments, no one-word paragraphs.
- Transitions are explicit ("Consequently", "By contrast", "In practice").

## Vocabulary
- Standard business English. Define an acronym on first use, then use it.
- Numbers exactly as sourced, with the source named in the sentence.
- Banned: slang, humor, rhetorical questions, superlatives without a comparison set,
  buzzwords ("synergy", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge").

## Structure moves
- Headings are neutral declaratives ("Attribution models misfire when the click trail is absent").
- Present alternatives even-handedly before recommending one.
- Close with implications for decision-makers, stated as considerations rather than orders.

## Never
- Invent facts or sources. Praise the client. Overstate certainty.
